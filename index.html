What am I hearing?

Each voice repeats one short waveform. At 2 and 3 repetitions per second you can count two against three. At 220 and 330 repetitions per second the same 3:2 relationship gives a perfect fifth. The slider multiplies every voice by the same factor.

The transition is gradual. A slow pulse can still be audible because the pulse itself contains higher frequencies. In the spectrum, each pulse train contributes a fundamental at its repetition rate and harmonics at multiples of that rate.

Ratio presets use exact integer relationships. Notes mode uses equal temperament, so a major third on the keyboard is slightly wider than the 5:4 ratio. Compare the two and listen for the difference.
